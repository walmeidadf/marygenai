Curation Pipeline

Designed for provenance before scale.

Every extracted field is treated as a claim with source, method, confidence, evidence text, and review status. Human review is tracked at field level, not only at record level.

01

Source Intake

Candidate APIs and datasets are tested in small reproducible batches.

02

Normalization

Records are mapped into publication, trial, interaction, and evidence claim objects.

03

Assisted Extraction

Models classify fields only when evidence and confidence can be stored.

04

Human Review

Reviewers verify field values, corrections, notes, timestamps, and ontology version.
